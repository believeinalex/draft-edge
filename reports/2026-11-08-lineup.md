# Lineup optimizer — week of 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-15

Run 2026-11-08. Standings base 2026-11-02 (+ games through stats 2026-11-08 + expected 2026-11-08 from locked lineups); injuries 2026-11-08; lineups 2026-11-02; 4000 simulations.

**Season-end Δ** is the change in expected roto points at the end of the season versus the carried-over lineup (this week's locked actives, filled to 9). One week is a small slice of the season, so these numbers are small. The ± is simulation noise only; differences under about 0.1 are within the model’s own error, so treat them as coin flips. **Week-end Δ** scores next Sunday's standings instead, which overreacts when teams are bunched in a category: use it to see which categories move, not for the size of the edge.

## Model

Per-game rates blend players.js projections with season-to-date stats (season weight gp / (gp + 20); `--prior=` changes it). Healthy players play a scheduled game with probability 0.95; P 0.9, Q 0.5, O/D 0 (0.7 from an ESPN return date). Weekly role factor sd 0.12; rest-of-season player error sd 0.2, FG%/FT% sd 0.02/0.04. Opponents keep their current locked actives (filled to 9). Rest of season: each team's top 9 by projection × availability, over its NBA team's remaining games. Constants are in `LINEUP_MODEL` in `season/lineup_optimizer.js`.
