# Buy-low targets — 2026-11-24

**Method.** Candidates are players ranked 180th or better in the preseason projections (`players.js` as of the 2026-11-04 load, before the 2026-11-20 blend absorbed early-season slumps) who are now **20+ ranks worse** in Hashtag's season-to-date per-game value (stats through 2026-11-23). There are 66 of them. For each, shooting was compared with the player's **last three seasons** from ESPN (3P% needs attempts, which the local stats don't have, so 3PA comes from ESPN box scores for every game since Oct 20). **Regressed rank** re-scores his current line with FG%, 3P% and FT% at the three-year rates, holding volume, minutes and counting stats fixed. That is the rank his line would have if his shooting were normal. A large gap between current and regressed rank with steady minutes means the slump is mostly shooting, which is the buy-low signal. Falling minutes means the role changed, which is not.

"Last 12 days" means games from Nov 12 to Nov 23.

## Caveats
- Regressed rank assumes shooting returns *fully* to the three-year rate. True rest-of-season talent sits somewhere between this season and the norm, so read regressed rank as a ceiling.
- Three-year rates come from ESPN season totals (2023-24 to 2025-26). Young players or those whose role has changed, such as Reid's starting minutes and Gobert's age, may have a new normal.
- Screen every trade for value on both sides (Hashtag's trade machine) per league rules.
